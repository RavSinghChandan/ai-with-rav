title: How to read an AI headline

@hook|'AI beats doctors' — **three questions first**

@image|images/short01-news.png|The headline, and the three questions under it

@beat|**Which test?** 1,000 clean textbook images, not a real OPD queue.

@beat|**Versus whom?** Junior residents, not senior radiologists.

@beat|**Did the data leak?** The test images were online. Often nobody checked.

@takeaway|Three questions turn most AI headlines into something much smaller.
