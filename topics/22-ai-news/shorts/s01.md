title: Read the benchmark, not the headline

@hook|'Beats humans at X' usually means: **on this test, under these conditions**.

@image|images/short01-news.png|The headline, and the three questions under it

@beat|Ask what the test actually measured — and who chose it.

@beat|Ask what it was compared against. Untrained humans? Experts?

@beat|Ask whether the test data leaked into training. Often nobody checked.

@takeaway|Three questions turn most AI headlines into something much smaller and more honest.
