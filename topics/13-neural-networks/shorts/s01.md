title: A neuron is a weighted vote
---

@hook|One neuron does three things: **multiply, add, decide**. That is it.

@image|images/short01-neuron.png|Multiply, add, decide

@beat|Each input is multiplied by a **weight** — how much that input matters.

@beat|The results are added together into a single number.

@beat|If that number crosses a threshold, the neuron **fires**.

@takeaway|Learning is nothing more than adjusting those weights until the votes come out right.
