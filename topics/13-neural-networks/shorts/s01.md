title: What one neuron actually does

@hook|A neuron does **three things**. That is all.

@image|images/short01-neuron.png|Multiply, add, decide

@beat|Multiply each input by a **weight** — how much that input matters.

@beat|Add the results into one number. Income 0.9, credit 0.7, loans 0.4 → **0.61**.

@beat|Over the threshold? It **fires**. Loan approved.

@takeaway|Learning is only this: nudging weights until the answers come out right.
