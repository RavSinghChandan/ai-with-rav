title: A graph lets the agent go backwards
---

@hook|A chain only moves forward. A **graph** can loop back and try again.

@image|images/short01-graph.png|One direction versus a loop that can retry

@beat|Chain: prompt → model → answer. One direction, always.

@beat|Graph: if the answer fails a check, **route back** and retry differently.

@beat|That loop is what makes 'agentic' behaviour possible at all.

@takeaway|If your flow never needs to go back, you do not need a graph yet.
