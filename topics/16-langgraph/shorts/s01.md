title: Chain vs graph, explained
---

@hook|A chain **cannot go backwards**. A graph can.

@image|images/short01-graph.png|One direction versus a loop that can retry

@beat|Chain: prompt → model → answer. One direction, always.

@beat|Graph: if the answer fails a check, **route back** and try differently.

@beat|That loop is what makes agentic behaviour possible at all.

@takeaway|If your flow never needs to retry, you do not need a graph yet.
