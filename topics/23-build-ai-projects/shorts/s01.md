@hook|Your first AI project should do **one task for one person** — and actually run.

@image|images/short01-ship.png|Notebook versus something someone used

@beat|A tutorial notebook proves you can follow steps. Nothing more.

@beat|Something deployed, that another person used, proves you can finish.

@beat|Write down honestly what it does badly. Almost nobody does this.

@takeaway|That honest limitations note is the strongest thing in an AI portfolio.
