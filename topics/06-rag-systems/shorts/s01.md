title: RAG is an open-book exam
---

@hook|A model without RAG sits the exam from memory. With RAG, it gets **the book**.

@image|images/short01-rag.png|Retrieve first, then answer from what was retrieved

@beat|Your question first **searches your documents** for the relevant pages.

@beat|Those pages are pasted into the prompt alongside your question.

@beat|The model answers from what is in front of it, not from memory.

@takeaway|That is why RAG cuts hallucination — you changed the exam, not the student.
