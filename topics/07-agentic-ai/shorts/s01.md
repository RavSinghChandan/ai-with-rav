@hook|Most 'AI agents' are just **for-loops**

@image|images/short01-agent.png|Fixed path versus the model choosing

@beat|An agent is a model that **decides what to do next**. That is the definition.

@beat|Fixed order every single time? That is a pipeline with a bigger bill.

@beat|Agents earn their cost when the next step **depends on what came back**.

@takeaway|If you can draw the steps on paper first, you do not need an agent.
