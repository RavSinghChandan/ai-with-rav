title: When you do NOT need an agent
---

@hook|If you can **draw the steps on paper** before running it, you need a function, not an agent.

@image|images/short01-agent.png|Fixed path versus the model choosing

@beat|An agent is a model that **decides what to do next**. That is the whole definition.

@beat|Fixed order every time? That is a pipeline with extra billing.

@beat|Agents earn their cost when the next step **depends on what came back**.

@takeaway|Most 'agent' demos are for-loops. Know which one you are building.
