title: 99% accurate and useless
---

@hook|If 99 of 100 transactions are genuine, saying **'genuine' every time** is 99% accurate.

@image|images/short01-accuracy.png|990 right, 10 missed — and the 10 were the point

@beat|That model catches **zero** fraud. It has learned nothing.

@beat|**Recall** asks: of all the real fraud, how much did I catch? Here, none.

@beat|Accuracy hid it because 990 easy answers drowned 10 expensive mistakes.

@takeaway|On imbalanced data, accuracy is the metric that makes a useless model look excellent.
