title: Why 99% accuracy can be worthless
---

@hook|**99% accurate. Catches zero fraud.**

@image|images/short01-accuracy.png|990 right, 10 missed — and the 10 were the point

@beat|1,000 transactions. 10 are fraud. Predict 'not fraud' every single time.

@beat|Accuracy: **99%**. Fraud caught: **zero**. The model learned nothing.

@beat|**Recall** asks the real question: of all the fraud, how much did I catch?

@takeaway|On imbalanced data, accuracy makes a useless model look excellent.
