title: The prompt fix nobody teaches

@hook|One **example** beats three paragraphs of rules

@image|images/short01-fewshot.png|Instructions guess; examples pin it down

@beat|'Reply politely and formally' gets you 'Dear Esteemed Customer, we are in receipt...'

@beat|Show one example instead: 'order late?' → 'Sorry sir, aapka order kal tak aa jayega.'

@beat|Now it matches your tone, your length and your language.

@takeaway|When a prompt is not working, add an example before adding more words.
