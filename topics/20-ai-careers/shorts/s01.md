title: Why 'I know Python' is not enough
---

@hook|Every AI job asks for Python. **None of them hire you for Python.**

@image|images/short01-layers3.png|Most stop at layer 1 and apply anyway

@beat|**Tools** — Python, an API call, a notebook. Everyone applying has this.

@beat|**Judgement** — which model, and why. This is where interviews happen.

@beat|**Evidence** — something running that another person actually used.

@takeaway|One deployed project you can defend beats ten tutorials you followed.
