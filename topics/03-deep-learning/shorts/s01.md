title: What 'deep' actually means
---

@hook|Deep does not mean clever. It means **layers**, each one seeing a bigger picture.

@image|images/short01-layers.png|Edges become shapes become objects

@beat|Layer 1 sees **edges**. Just light and dark boundaries.

@beat|Layer 2 combines edges into **shapes** — a curve, a corner.

@beat|Layer 3 combines shapes into **things** — an eye, a wheel, a letter.

@takeaway|Nobody programmed those stages. The network found them because depth forces it to.
