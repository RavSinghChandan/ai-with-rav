title: What 'deep' in deep learning actually means
---

@hook|'Deep' does not mean clever. It means **layers**.

@image|images/short01-layers.png|Edges become shapes become objects

@beat|Layer 1 sees only **edges** — light meeting dark.

@beat|Layer 2 combines those edges into **shapes**.

@beat|Layer 3 combines shapes into **things**: an eye, a wheel, a letter.

@takeaway|Nobody programmed those stages. Depth forced the network to find them.
