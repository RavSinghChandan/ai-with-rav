@hook|AI does **not understand you**. It matches patterns.

@image|images/short01-understand.png|Pattern, not understanding

@beat|It has read 'The capital of Bihar is Patna' millions of times.

@beat|So it answers Patna, correctly, every time.

@beat|Now ask about a village nobody wrote about. **It still answers confidently.**

@takeaway|The SHAPE of an answer is all it knows. You stay responsible for the facts.
