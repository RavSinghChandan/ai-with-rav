title: A signal is a value that tells you it changed
---

@hook|The old way: you tell the view to update. The **signal** way: it already knows.

@image|images/short01-signal.png|The value notifies its watchers

@beat|A signal holds a value and a **list of who is watching it**.

@beat|Change the value and every watcher is notified automatically.

@beat|No manual change detection, no wondering why the screen is stale.

@takeaway|This is the same idea as a spreadsheet cell. Change one, the rest recalculate.
