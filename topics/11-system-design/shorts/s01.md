title: What to cache and what never to cache
---

@hook|Cache the wrong thing. **Serve stale data.**

@image|images/short01-cache.png|Slow and repeated is worth caching; nothing else is

@beat|IRCTC train list, Delhi to Patna: changes daily, asked 50,000 times an hour. **Cache it.**

@beat|Live seat availability: asked just as often, but changes **every second**. Never cache.

@beat|Your booking history: barely changes, but asked **once per login**. Not worth it.

@takeaway|Slow to compute AND often repeated AND stays valid. All three, or don't.
