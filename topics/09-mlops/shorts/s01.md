@hook|Nothing in the code changed. The **world** changed. That is data drift.

@image|images/short01-drift.png|Same model, shifting world, falling accuracy

@beat|A model trained on 2023 shopping habits meets 2026 shoppers.

@beat|Accuracy falls quietly — no error, no crash, no alert.

@beat|You only find out when someone complains about the results.

@takeaway|Monitor the inputs, not just uptime. Drift is silent by nature.
