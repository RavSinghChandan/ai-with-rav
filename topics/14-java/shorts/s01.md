title: == compares boxes, .equals compares contents

@hook|Two Strings that look identical can still be **not equal** in Java. Here is why.

@image|images/short01-equals.png|Same box, or same contents?

@beat|`==` asks: are these the **same object in memory**?

@beat|`.equals()` asks: do they **hold the same value**?

@beat|Small strings get reused by the JVM, so `==` sometimes works — by accident.

@takeaway|Use .equals() for values. The times == works are luck, not logic.
