@beat|The output of one step is the input of the next. That is a chain.

@beat|The real value is the **plumbing**: retries, parsing, swapping models.

@beat|You could write this yourself. The library saves the boring parts.

@takeaway|Learn the pattern, not the library. The pattern outlives every framework.
