title: A chain is just a pipeline

@hook|LangChain's core idea is small: **the output of one step is the input of the next**.

@image|images/short01-chain.png|Output of one step feeds the next

@beat|Prompt → model → parse → next prompt. Nothing magical.

@beat|The value is the **plumbing**: retries, parsing, swapping models.

@beat|You can write this yourself. The library saves the boring parts.

@takeaway|Learn the pattern, not the library. The pattern outlives every framework.
