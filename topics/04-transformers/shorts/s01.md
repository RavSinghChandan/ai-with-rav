title: Attention, in one sentence

@hook|Attention lets every word **look at every other word** before deciding what it means.

@image|images/short01-attention.png|The same word, pulled two ways by its neighbours

@beat|'The **bank** was steep' and 'The **bank** was closed' — same word, different meaning.

@beat|The model reads the whole sentence and **weights** which other words matter.

@beat|'Steep' pulls *bank* toward the river. 'Closed' pulls it toward money.

@takeaway|That weighting is attention. It is why context finally worked.
