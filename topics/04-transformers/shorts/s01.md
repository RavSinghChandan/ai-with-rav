@hook|Same word. **Two completely different meanings.**

@image|images/short01-attention.png|The same word, pulled two ways by its neighbours

@beat|'The **bank** was steep.' 'The **bank** was closed.'

@beat|The model reads the whole sentence and **weights** which words matter.

@beat|'Steep' pulls it toward the river. 'Closed' pulls it toward money.

@takeaway|That weighting is attention. It is why context finally worked.
