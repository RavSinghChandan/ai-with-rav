title: Why a dot product is just agreement
---

@hook|A dot product asks one question: **are these two arrows pointing the same way?**

@image|images/short01-agreement.png|Same direction, no direction, opposite direction

@beat|Two vectors pointing the **same way** give a big positive number.

@beat|**At right angles** gives zero — no agreement at all.

@beat|**Opposite ways** gives a negative number. That is the whole intuition.

@takeaway|Every similarity score in AI — search, recommendations, embeddings — is this one question.
