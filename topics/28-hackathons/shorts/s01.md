title: What hackathon judges actually ask
---

@hook|The demo does not win. **The questions do.**

@beat|Everyone can demo. The screen moves, numbers appear, it looks alive.

@beat|Then one judge asks: **'where did that number come from?'**

@beat|My own app printed +6.2%. It was `random.uniform()` against a fixed table.

@takeaway|If a number on your screen is a guess, say so — and show the formula.
