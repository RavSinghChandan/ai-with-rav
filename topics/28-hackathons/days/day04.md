@callout|yellow|In One Line: A click becomes a lead. Only some leads become sales. Skip that step and your maths becomes fantasy.

@h2|The shortcut that breaks everything
Most demo funnels go:

**clicks → conversions → revenue**

The missing middle: a click gives you a *lead* — an enquiry, a form, a call.
Someone still has to close it.

@image|images/img04-funnel-gap.png|The step that goes missing: most leads never become sales, and the gap is where the money is

@h2|What it did to my numbers
My app implied a coaching student acquired for about **₹583** against a
**₹3,000** course fee. A 5x return, effortlessly.

Any agency owner watching would have stopped listening right there. Acquiring a
paying student for ₹583 is not a good campaign — it is a wrong calculation.

@h2|Real close rates are brutal
@bullets
Real estate — about **6 in 100** leads close. It is a years-long decision.
Coaching — about **18 in 100**. Cheaper, faster, still a real decision.
Ecommerce — about **85 in 100**, because the click *is* the purchase.
@end

Adding that one step moved my cost-per-sale from ₹583 to **₹3,571** — a figure a
real marketer would recognise.

@callout|red|If your funnel has no drop-off between click and sale, you are not modelling a business. You are modelling a wish.
