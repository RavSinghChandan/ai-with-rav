title: Memory that fades
---

@hook|An RNN reads a sentence word by word, carrying a **memory** forward. That memory leaks.

@image|images/short01-memory.png|Early words fade as the sentence grows

@beat|Each word updates a small hidden state passed to the next step.

@beat|By word fifty, the influence of word one has almost vanished.

@beat|That fading is the vanishing gradient — the reason attention was invented.

@takeaway|RNNs taught us sequence matters. Transformers fixed the forgetting.
