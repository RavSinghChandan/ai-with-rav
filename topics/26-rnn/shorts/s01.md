@hook|By word 50, word 1 is **almost gone**

@image|images/short01-memory.png|Early words fade as the sentence grows

@beat|'Ravi, who grew up in Patna and later moved to Pune, speaks ___'

@beat|Each word updates a small memory passed to the next step.

@beat|By the blank, 'Patna' is down to **6%**. It cannot tell you Hindi.

@takeaway|That fading is the vanishing gradient. Attention was invented to fix it.
