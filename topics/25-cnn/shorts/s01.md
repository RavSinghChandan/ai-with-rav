title: How a CNN actually sees an image

@hook|A CNN never looks at the **whole image**

@image|images/short01-filter.png|One small window, slid across everything

@beat|It slides a window a few pixels wide, looking for **one pattern**.

@beat|On flat colour: nothing. Straddling a boundary: **edge found**.

@beat|Stack many windows and you detect many patterns at once.

@takeaway|The SAME small window slides everywhere. That is why CNNs need so little data.
