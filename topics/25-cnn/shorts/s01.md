title: A filter is a tiny sliding window
---

@hook|A CNN does not look at the whole image. It slides a **small window** over it.

@image|images/short01-filter.png|One small window, slid across everything

@beat|The window is a few pixels wide and looks for one pattern — say a vertical edge.

@beat|It slides across the entire image, marking where that pattern appears.

@beat|Stack many windows and you detect many patterns at once.

@takeaway|Sliding the same small filter everywhere is why CNNs need so little data per feature.
