@hook|ChatGPT is **not thinking**. Here is what happens.

@image|images/short01-nextword.png|One word at a time, each one fed back in

@beat|Given 'The capital of Maharashtra is', it scores **every word it knows**.

@beat|'Mumbai' scores **0.94**. It gets picked. Then the whole thing repeats.

@beat|Fluency is the **side effect** of doing that billions of times.

@takeaway|Knowing this tells you exactly when it will confidently invent something.
