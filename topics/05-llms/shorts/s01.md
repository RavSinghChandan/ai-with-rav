title: An LLM only predicts the next word
---

@hook|ChatGPT is not thinking. It is answering: **what word comes next?** — again and again.

@image|images/short01-nextword.png|One word at a time, each one fed back in

@beat|Given 'The capital of France is', it scores every word it knows.

@beat|'Paris' scores highest, so it is picked. Then the whole thing repeats.

@beat|Fluency is the **side effect** of doing that billions of times.

@takeaway|Knowing this tells you exactly when it will confidently invent something.
