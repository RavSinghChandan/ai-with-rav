@hook|Wrong question: **which AI tool is best?**

@image|images/short01-risk.png|Cheap mistakes and expensive mistakes need different tools

@beat|Drafting a WhatsApp reply? A wrong answer costs you **ten seconds**.

@beat|Summarising a rent agreement? A wrong answer costs you **a clause**.

@beat|Same model. Same speed. Completely different amount of checking.

@takeaway|Match the effort to the cost of the mistake, not to the hype.
