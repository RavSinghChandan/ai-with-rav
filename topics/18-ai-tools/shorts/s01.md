title: Pick the tool by the failure you can afford
---

@hook|The question is not which tool is best. It is **what happens when it is wrong**.

@image|images/short01-risk.png|Cheap mistakes and expensive mistakes need different tools

@beat|Drafting an email? A wrong answer costs you ten seconds.

@beat|Summarising a contract? A wrong answer costs you a clause.

@beat|The second case needs citation and review. The first does not.

@takeaway|Match the checking effort to the cost of the mistake, not to the hype.
