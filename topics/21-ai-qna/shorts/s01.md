title: Why it invents citations
---

@hook|A made-up reference is not a lie. It is the model doing **exactly what it was built to do**.

@image|images/short01-citation.png|Plausible shape, no lookup step

@beat|It predicts text that *looks* like a citation — author, year, journal.

@beat|A plausible-looking fake scores as well as a real one during prediction.

@beat|It has no lookup step, so nothing checks whether the paper exists.

@takeaway|Never accept a citation you have not opened. This failure is structural.
