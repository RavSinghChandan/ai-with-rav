title: Finish beats perfect

@hook|An unfinished impressive project teaches you **less** than a finished simple one.

@image|images/short01-finish.png|Where the learning actually is

@beat|Finishing forces the boring parts: errors, edge cases, deployment.

@beat|Those boring parts are exactly what interviews ask about.

@beat|A perfect half-project has no boring parts, so it teaches nothing.

@takeaway|Scope down until you can finish this week. Then do it again.
